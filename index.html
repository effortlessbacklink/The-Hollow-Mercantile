The Hollow Mercantile

About

The Hollow Mercantile is a small-batch apothecary in Saucier, Mississippi, creating handmade herbal skincare, bath products, tallow balms, personal care items, and dog grooming goods. The brand is inspired by traditional remedies and simple ingredients rather than changing trends.

Products are made by hand in small batches using ingredients such as grass-fed tallow and slow-infused herbal oils. Visit The Hollow Mercantile to explore collections including Whipped Tallow Balms, Pocket Apothecary, Hollow Hound for dogs, and seasonal releases.

The Hollow Mercantile also offers an ingredient library that helps customers understand what each ingredient does. With a focus on thoughtful formulas and time-tested ingredients, the brand brings traditional apothecary care into everyday routines.
